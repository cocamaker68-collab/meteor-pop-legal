1. Information handled by the game

Meteor Pop saves gameplay progress, earned in-game currency, unlocked items, achievements, and settings locally on your device. The game does not require you to register an account or provide your name or email address to play.

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), which may automatically collect and share the following information with Google to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent fraud:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general or approximate location;
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views;
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as app launch times, crashes, hangs, and resource use;
  • Device and other identifiers, including Android advertising ID or app set ID when available and permitted.

Actual collection may vary based on device settings, region, applicable law, consent status, and SDK configuration. We do not claim that denying personalized-ad consent necessarily prevents all SDK data processing.

2. Purposes of processing

We use or allow our advertising provider to process information to display ads, measure advertising performance, analyze technical performance, prevent invalid traffic and fraud, and comply with applicable obligations. Locally stored game data is used to maintain your progress and preferences.

3. Third-party provider and sharing

We use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to provide rewarded and interstitial ads. Google may receive the information described above as a third-party service provider. Read Google's policies for details:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

We do not sell your locally stored gameplay progress. No other third-party data collection is described here; this statement must be updated if additional SDKs or services are introduced.

4. Advertising choices and consent

Where required, Meteor Pop uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent. If the applicable privacy choices interface is required for your region, you can access it through the game's Settings → Privacy Options control. You may also manage or reset your Android advertising ID through Android device settings. Available choices depend on region and applicable laws.

5. Data security

The Google Mobile Ads SDK states that it encrypts transmitted user information using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Local game saves remain on your device. No online system is guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to use supported SDK configurations and reasonable safeguards.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data is kept on your device until it is cleared, deleted through an available in-game reset function, or removed when your device or app installation deletes the app's data. The retention and deletion of information processed by Google is governed by Google's applicable privacy and retention policies. For privacy requests involving our game, contact us using the address below; we may not be able to directly identify or delete data held independently by Google.

7. Children and intended audience

Meteor Pop is designed for a general gaming audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request children's names, contact details, or accounts. Advertising and age-related handling will follow the final audience declaration and applicable Google Play policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us.

8. International processing

Because Google operates internationally, information processed by its services may be transferred to or processed in countries outside your location, subject to the provider's policies and applicable safeguards.
